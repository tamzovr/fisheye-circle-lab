English

Data Collection

Fisheye Circle Lab does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising data, or tracking information.

Video and Image Processing

Videos and images selected by the user are processed locally on the Mac. Fisheye Circle Lab does not upload these files to an external server.

Accounts and Advertising

The app does not require an account and does not display advertising. It does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Exported Files

Files exported by the user, including JSON, CSV, and PNG files, are saved to locations selected by the user.

Support Email

If you contact support by email, your email address and the information you include in your message are used only to respond to and manage your support request.

Contact

fisheyecirclelab.support@gmail.com
